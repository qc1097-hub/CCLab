Welcome to Friendship Donut!

In this interactive storytelling game, you will help four long-lost friends plan a long-awaited reunion by playing a role.

Click the glowing donut pieces on the homepage to enter different tasks. If you feel stuck, use the Hint button to get new clues.

Throughout the game, you will help them pick a reunion date, gather items to recreate an old photo, buy their favorite donuts from student days, and finally take a photo for them at the reunion.

Most importantly — no matter when you're playing this game, don't forget to reach out to your friends too!
