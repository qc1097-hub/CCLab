Hint

The marked dates on each calendar mean that person is not available.

The notes on the right explain what they are doing on those days.

For example, in Calendar 1, Dec 1-5 are marked because she has class, so only Dec 6 and 7 are free for her.

Using the same logic, compare the unmarked dates across the other calendars to find the days when everyone is free.

If you need a stronger hint:

Calendars 1 & 2 share free dates on Dec 6, 20-28. Calendars 3 & 4 share free dates on Dec 6-7, 13, and 29-31.

After you find the correct date, click the matching date on the last calendar.

If you choose the right one, you'll automatically move to the next page.
